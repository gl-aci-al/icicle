Analytics

Icicle uses Umami for anonymous page-view analytics. It does not use cookies, collect personal information, or track you across other sites. Search parameters and URL fragments are excluded, and only page views are recorded.

You can opt out at any time with the "Analytics on" button in the top right of any page. Your choice is saved in your browser.

The extension

Icicle's filtering and classification run locally in your browser. Blocklists and settings stay on your device.
