About

What?

Icicle is a browser extension, and soon an Android app, for X and soon other social media. It helps people customize the site to their liking, filter out what they don't want, and adds small quality-of-life changes.

Why?

I originally built Icicle during August 2026 to avoid GTA VI spoilers and leaks, with Firefox help from swagdotsh and feature suggestions from a lot of cool friends of mine (Thank you! ❤️). As I realised the scope of the project, it made sense to brand Icicle as a tool to avoid any broad topic.

I have an issue, bug, or blocklist!

Open an issue on GitHub. You're also welcome to build a fix yourself and send a pull request (Vibe-coded, unclear, or nonfunctional pull requests won't be merged.).

Icicle didn't catch this post!

Icicle isn't guaranteed to catch every post covered by your blocklists. Bugs happen, and we will try to fix them if you make a bug report. Please include your config and blocklist, as well as the post that wasn't filtered.
